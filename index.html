01 — Profile

Resident energy.
Guest discipline.

Short version for bookers. Full bio for press.

DEV plays house and techno with weight. Low end first. Vocals only when they earn the space. The job is simple: keep the room moving without turning it into a trailer.

Working since 2016. Clubs, warehouses, festival tents, and private rooms that still need to feel like a night out. Preference is a 4–6 hour window — enough time to open, tighten, and close without rushing the last hour into a highlight reel.

Bookings through the form or bookings@djdev.com. Tech rider and photos sit in the links below once the files are in the assets folder.
